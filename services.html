Prohibited Items

What You Cannot Send

For the safety of our couriers and compliance with Bangladesh law, the following items are strictly prohibited.

Flammable Materials

Petrol, gas cylinders, fireworks, lighter fluid, paint thinners, and any combustible substances.

Illegal Items

Drugs narcotics, counterfeit currency, unlicensed pharmaceuticals, and any goods prohibited by Bangladesh law.

Cash & Jewelry

Large amounts of cash, gold, precious stones, and high-value luxury items above 50,000 BDT without insurance.

Weapons & Ammunition

Firearms, ammunition, knives, explosives, and any weapon or replica regardless of licensing status.

Perishable Food

Raw meat, dairy, and unpackaged food that may spoil during transit. Properly sealed dry foods are allowed.

Live Animals

Livestock, pets, poultry, and any living creature. We do not transport animals under any circumstances.

If you're unsure whether an item can be shipped, please contact us on WhatsApp before booking. Sending prohibited items may result in confiscation and legal action.
